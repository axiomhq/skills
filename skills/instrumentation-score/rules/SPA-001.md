**Rule ID:** SPA-001

**Description:** Traces contain a limited number of `INTERNAL` spans per service.

**Rationale:** Services producing an excessive number of internal spans may indicate inefficient or complex operations. This can impact observability and performance monitoring, making it harder to identify bottlenecks and troubleshoot issues.

**Target:** Span

**Criteria:** When grouping spans by trace identifier and `service.name`, no more than 10 spans in a single trace SHOULD have `span.kind = SpanKind.SPAN_KIND_INTERNAL`.

**Impact:** Normal

**Example Query:**

```apl
// Axiom stores SpanKind as lowercase strings in kind.
['otel-demo-traces']
| summarize
    observed=count(),
    internal_spans=countif(kind == 'internal')
  by trace_id, ['service.name']
| summarize
    observed_spans=sum(observed),
    trace_service_groups=count(),
    internal_spans=sum(internal_spans),
    max_internal_spans=max(internal_spans),
    violating_groups=countif(internal_spans > 10)
  by ['service.name']
```
